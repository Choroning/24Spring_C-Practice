## 📝 About This Repository

- `Lecture-Notes/` holds a lecture note and a lab note for each chapter, and Chapters 1-10 also hold exercises made up of the textbook and lab problems, with their solutions.
- `Coursework/` holds the assignments of Computer Language and Training Ⅰ (`HW01`-`HW10`) and the chapter lab code (`Lab04`-`Lab10`).
- Units follow the textbook's chapter numbers (`Ch01`-`Ch10`), and every document comes as an English (`.md`) and Korean (`.ko.md`) pair.

> **🤖 AI Agent Usage**
> Computer Language and Training Ⅰ required students to write their own code.
> [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/download) was used to organize and translate the notes and to restructure the repository.

### Course Overview

Two general studies courses on the fundamentals of computer programming in C. Computer Language Ⅰ is a theory course that follows the textbook through the basic concepts of C, and Computer Language and Training Ⅰ is its linked lab course. The instructors, schedule, assessment, course policies, and progress are in the [course information document](Course-Information.md).
